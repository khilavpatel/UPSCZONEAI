The alternatives

Three options aspirants compare us with

Vajiram, Vision, Drishti

Traditional offline

400+ students in one hall. Fees above ₹2.2 lakh plus ₹20,000 a month living costs. Copies come back in about 15 days with two lines of notes.

  • ₹2.5L to ₹4.5L total annual expense
  • 14–20 days delay in Mains copies
  • No personalized 1-on-1 daily guidance
ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity

Generic chatbots

Trained on general data, with a flattering tone and no grasp of UPSC marking schemes, Intro-Body-Way-Forward rules, 2nd ARC or Indian Supreme Court rulings.

  • Can hallucinate Indian legal and constitutional articles
  • No handwriting OCR for exam sheets
  • No 30-year Prelims elimination logic
SuperKalam, SudoLearn, PrepAI

New AI startups

Built quickly on general APIs. Daily answer quotas, credit deductions and queues during exam peaks.

  • Credit costs of ₹12k–₹18k a year
  • Lacks diagram evaluation
  • No full GS-4 Ethics case-study frameworks
